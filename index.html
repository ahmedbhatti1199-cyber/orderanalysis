OrderAnalysis

A private reporting tool for a small online business.

OrderAnalysis is used only by its owner to keep the business's own books: it reads the owner's ledger spreadsheet and the transaction notification emails that the owner's payment cards send, and turns them into profit and loss, cash and cost reports on the owner's computer.

It is not a public service. It has no sign-up and no other users, and it is not offered to anyone else. Its Google sign-in is used only by the owner, for the owner's own Google account.

Read the privacy policy to see what data it reads and how it is handled.
