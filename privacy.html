Privacy policy

OrderAnalysis · last updated 9 November 2026

OrderAnalysis is a private tool that the owner of a small online business uses to keep the business's own books. This policy covers its Google sign-ins (the ledger reader and the email reader). The tool is used only by its owner, with the owner's own Google account; it has no other users.

Google data it reads

  • Google Sheets, read only (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/spreadsheets.readonly): the tabs of the owner's business ledger spreadsheet.
  • Gmail, read only (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly): the transaction notification emails that the owner's payment cards and wallets send (payments, declined transactions and their fees). Other emails are not stored.

The tool never writes, sends, changes or deletes anything in Google Sheets or Gmail.

How the data is used

The data is used only to produce the owner's own profit and loss, cash and cost reports. From the emails it keeps only the transaction details (date, merchant, amount, fees and the card's last four digits).

Where it is kept

On the owner's own computer. The sign-in token is stored there too. Nothing is sent to any server run for this tool, and there is none.

Sharing

The data is not sold, shared or transferred to anyone, is not used for advertising, and is not used to train any AI or machine-learning model.

OrderAnalysis's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time in the Google account's third-party access settings; the stored data can be deleted from the owner's computer at any time.

Contact

Questions about this policy: the support email shown on the app's Google sign-in screen.
